In this sacred first episode, I invited my good friend Daniel, who started dabbling in stocks in high school and day-traded during classes in his freshman year of college, to talk about the ideals and realities of studying finance, and the bittersweet experiences of working and learning at an investment firm.

Finally, I also asked Daniel to offer some advice to junior students who aspire to become traders: what qualities are essential for a stock market trader, and in what directions should they strive to improve.
