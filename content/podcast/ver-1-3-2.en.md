Continuing the hard-core career conversation from the last episode, this installment of "Life Release Notes" completely shifts gears to a "Rational Logic vs. Occult Universe" challenge arena! We've once again invited our friend Jun, who is currently working in consulting and was also the former president of the NTU Tarot Club, to chat about how he entered the field of metaphysics and the operational logic behind Tarot and face reading.

As a science-minded individual steeped in data science, who demands evidence for everything, I couldn't help but go all out, unleashing a barrage of engineer's questions:
*   **Is fortune-telling a form of Overfitting?** Drawing a Tarot card daily and trying to force connections – isn't that just overfitting in machine learning?
*   **If you tamper with the Input Data, will the model break?** If face reading can predict one's fortune, then getting hyaluronic acid injections or removing moles via cosmetic procedures – wouldn't that invalidate the prediction model?
*   **The randomness and ritualistic nature of AI fortune-telling:** If an AI draws a Tarot card for you, at its core, it's just a random function with a Random Seed. Does that still hold any meaning for fortune-telling?
*   **Manipulating the fortune-telling market with SEO:** If I use an LLM combined with the strongest SEO algorithm, could I then manipulate the recommendation weights in the fortune-telling market?

How will Jun interpret these relentless "data science smashes"? If you're also someone who values logic in everything, or if you have an endless curiosity about metaphysics, this wildly out-of-control paradox showdown episode will definitely keep you captivated!
