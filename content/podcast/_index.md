# 人生 release note

人生 release note 是一個半訪談、半閒聊的頻道。  
datafox 目前是準 Google 實習生，但身邊有各式各樣走向不同道路的好朋友。  

這裡和「強者我朋友」那種已經很穩定、很強大的訪談不太一樣，我專門訪問那些還在探索、還在 debug、還在找下一個可能方向的人。  

我們想用現在的想法和聲音，為下一個 released version 蓋穩台階，也讓正在走相似道路的同儕與學弟妹，看見從大學畢業到進入職場這段時間，作為 beta 版的我們，下一個想嘗試的 branch。

如果有任何想要查詢的單集或者是你感興趣的關鍵字，都可以使用上方的「搜尋你想要找尋的關鍵字」欄位進行搜尋，這將會是一個用exact match搜尋，可以快速找到關鍵字並即時顯示的功能，歡迎大家試用。另外，如果有任何對我的podcast有興趣，想要來和datafox聊聊自己的人生beta版決策的話，歡迎寄信給 datafox.tw@gmail.com，我很歡迎和各種不同領域的夥伴一起冒險、探索人生中可能非最佳但最獨一無二的經歷。

除此之外，如果你有具體的職涯迷惘或技術問題想找這隻狐狸聊聊，我也提供 『Coffee Chat 預約』。這是一個免費的交流時段，如果你覺得聊完有收穫，歡迎透過捐款給 Sunny Fox Family 表達感謝，讓這份能量循環給真正的狐狸。

# Life Release Note

Life Release Note is a channel that is half interview, half casual chat. datafox is currently a prospective Google intern but is surrounded by good friends pursuing diverse paths.

Unlike interviews with "my accomplished friends" who are already very stable and powerful, I specialize in interviewing those who are still exploring, still debugging, and still searching for their next possible direction.

We hope to use our current thoughts and voices to build a solid foundation for the next released version, and to allow peers and juniors on similar paths to see the next branch that we, as beta versions, want to try during the period from university graduation to entering the workplace.

If you have any episodes you'd like to look up or keywords you're interested in, you can use the "Search for keywords you want to find" field above to search. This feature will use exact match search to quickly find keywords and display them instantly. Everyone is welcome to try it out. Additionally, if you are interested in my podcast and would like to chat with datafox about your life's beta version decisions, please feel free to email datafox.tw@gmail.com. I welcome venturing and exploring potentially non-optimal but truly unique life experiences with partners from various fields.

Furthermore, if you have specific career confusion or technical questions you'd like to discuss with this fox, I also offer 'Coffee Chat Appointments'. This is a free exchange session. If you find the conversation beneficial, feel free to express your gratitude by donating to Sunny Fox Family, allowing this energy to cycle back to the real foxes.
