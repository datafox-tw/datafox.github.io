## 🦊 Let’s Chat with Datafox

If you’ve landed on this page, you’re likely at a career crossroads, or perhaps you’re just looking to talk to someone who’s equally skeptical and passionate about technology and life.

### Why talk to me?
To be fair, I’m just a student—roughly **273 months old**—still figuring things out. However, I’ve "accidentally" gathered a pretty interesting collection of experiences along the way:
*   **From Finance to Data Science:** I know exactly what that "Who am I? Where am I? Why is my code actually working?" anxiety feels like when pivoting careers.
*   **Google Internship & Hackathons:** Having navigated the corporate interview gauntlet and won a few hackathons, I can share the "survival rules" of these systems.
*   **Research & Development:** My focus is on LLMs and Agentic Systems. If you want to dive into RAG or AI Safety, I’ll be all ears (and might not stop talking).

### What can we talk about?
*   **Career Pivots:** How to break into Data Science from a business background without losing your mind.
*   **Tech Exploration:** LLMs, Multi-Agent systems, or that one AI metric you’re currently suspicious of (we can deconstruct it together).
*   **Study Abroad & Exchange:** Life lessons from my time in Barcelona, or how to maintain a semblance of work-life balance while grinding through a Master's.
*   **Life in Beta:** Any ideas that are still in the "experimental" phase. I’m happy to provide a different—and perhaps critical—perspective.

### The Format
*   **Duration:** A default **30-minute** slot. If we're vibing, I'm more than happy to extend it over another cup of coffee.
*   **Location:** Primarily via **Google Meet**. However, if you happen to be in Taipei, I’m down for an in-person chat—face-to-face banter always has more soul.
*   **Language:** 中文 (Mandarin) or English. Whatever you're most comfortable with.

### Regarding "Payment"
This Coffee Chat is **completely free**. 

I don't need you to buy me a coffee. Instead, if you find our session insightful or feel it saved you a few wrong turns, I invite you to turn that gratitude into a small donation to **animal welfare organizations** (especially those protecting foxes or stray animals). Let’s pass that energy on to the furry lives that need it more than we do. I'll provide more details after our chat, but don't worry—it’s absolutely not mandatory!
