Today's Thoughts
My pace of life has been a bit faster lately. I just received a call that brought about some emotional fluctuations.

Thank you to everyone who supported and disliked me along this journey. Sigh, truly, thank you, I'm suddenly a bit speechless. This all feels like a dream. Although I'm currently in a "cooling-off period" of 99% certainty (still waiting for the final document, after all), this expectation of truly putting technology into practice is enough for me to write this small record.

Calm down, stay calm. The remaining 1% is for my future self to fill.
