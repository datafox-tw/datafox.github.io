# 2. From 0 to Launch: Technical Construction Process

## 2.1 Purchasing a Domain (GoDaddy and Other Options)

I chose `datafox.tw`.

The purchase process isn't difficult, but there are a few things to note:

- The first year is usually cheaper (promotional price). I used GoDaddy for my purchase, and it cost 1390 NTD for the first two years. Amortized, that's less than two NTD per day, which I found very cost-effective.
- Remember to check renewal prices
- Disable unnecessary add-ons; only purchase them when you genuinely need them.
- Consider the brand positioning of .tw vs .com (I liked the name datafox, but datafox.com was already taken)

Why datafox? Although it was initially a name that just popped into my head (if you know me, my primary name during university was "blackwingedkite," and I used that name on Medium. Even earlier, for instance, the first GitHub name I created was "bonnieker," a combination of "bonnie," a little rabbit I liked at the time, and my surname "ker").

However, when choosing a site name, I realized something: these names were a bit too hard to remember. "Blackwingedkite" has 15 letters and represents a species, not a technology (and it might be a bit awkward if I wanted to look at cute black-winged kite photos in the future and found myself instead). The name "bonnieker" was cuter but was just a very basic combination of a first name and surname, making it very uninteresting (and it's weird for a guy to be called Bonnie, lol). So, when I was thinking of a new name, I decided on "datafox." It's catchy and easy to remember, fits my usual fondness for foxes, and ".tw" also represents my identity and national affiliation. After searching, I found this name was surprisingly uncommon (I only saw one relatively obscure Oracle project using it), so I decided on this name as the starting point for my personal brand.

# 3. AI Era SEO Design

This section is what I value the most.

## 3.1 Implementing JSON-LD Person Schema

I'm not just doing SEO for Google; I'm preparing for AI Search Agents.

I added:

- `@type: Person`
- name
- affiliation
- jobTitle
- knowsAbout
- sameAs

Purpose:

To enable search engines and AI systems to clearly identify:

- My technical boundaries
- My research directions
- My identity tags

For this part, you can ask your AI assistant to help generate precise Schema markup. I implemented this in `extend_head.html`, ensuring that this professional information exists not just within the article but also in the webpage's "machine-readable" layer. In the era of big data, self-positioning for AI should be as precise, clear, and structured as writing code.

Here is an example of the **Person Schema** I actually used:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Person",
  "name": "Yu-Chi Ko (柯宥圻)",
  "jobTitle": "AI Systems & LLMOps Engineer",
  "knowsAbout": [
    "Large Language Models (LLM)",
    "LLMOps",
    "Agentic AI",
    "GraphRAG",
    "Quantitative Finance"
  ],
  "email": "datafox.tw@gmail.com",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://github.com/datafox-tw",
    "https://linkedin.com/in/koyuchi"
  ]
}
```

> [!TIP]
> Structured data helps AI build your "knowledge graph." When someone queries technical keywords in an AI search engine, having clear Schema markup and links to your NTUAI background will give you a competitive advantage over regular webpages.

# 4. Professional Upgrade Completed in 5 Minutes

Professionalism often stems from "reducing friction for the other party."

## 4.1 Business Contact Form (Formspree)

I didn't write a backend.

I used Formspree:

- Frontend HTML forms
- Direct forwarding to email
- No server required

To prevent spam, I also added an invisible **Honeypot** field:

```html
<form action="https://formspree.io/f/your_id" method="POST">
  <!-- 防垃圾郵件欄位 -->
  <input type="text" name="_gotcha" style="display:none" />
  
  <label>您的姓名 / Name:</label>
  <input type="text" name="name" required>
  
  <label>電子郵件 / Email:</label>
  <input type="email" name="_replyto" required>
  
  <button type="submit">傳送訊息 / Send Message</button>
</form>
```
Just point the `action` to the ID Formspree provides you, no API integration needed, and you'll immediately start receiving emails.

# 5. Traffic and Trust Mechanisms

## 5.1 Google Analytics 4: Data-Driven Personal Brand Optimization

In traditional views, only e-commerce or large websites require data analysis. However, in the AI era, "data awareness" for personal websites is equally crucial.

My core goal in implementing GA4 is to capture **"what visitors are interested in."** For a personal website, GA4 is currently the most powerful and **completely free** tracking tool available. Even with only 100 visitors per month, you can observe data like a professional PM:

- **Precisely capture visitor interest**: By checking which pages have the highest click-through rates (e.g., "Portfolio" vs. "Life Notes"), I can understand which of my traits potential collaborators or HR truly care about.
- **Measure engagement depth and dwell time**: GA4's improved "average engagement time" is more valuable than traditional "dwell time." If a visitor spends over 3 minutes on my LLMOps notes, it signifies that the content successfully triggered deep reading, rather than just an accidental click.
- **Track interactive behavior**: I can set up tracking for actions like "clicking the booking button" or "clicking the GitHub link." For me, this is the "conversion" of a personal brand.

In modern themes like PaperMod, installing GA4 is as simple as breathing; just configure one line with your measurement ID in `hugo.yaml`:

```yaml
googleAnalytics: G-XXXXXXXXXX
```

> [!NOTE]
> For personal websites with less than tens of thousands of monthly visitors, GA4 is virtually free for life. It allows you to have the same level of analytical insight as top tech companies, without needing to invest capital, helping you quickly iterate on the content of your "About Me" page.

## 5.2 Busuanzi Visitor Counter

I added a real-time visitor count display to transparently show traffic. This was the most delightful part for me because it truly requires no setup; you just need to insert two lines of code anywhere to make it work. It was a pleasant surprise, and it allows me to casually observe if my traffic is growing.

```html
<span id="busuanzi_container_site_pv">| Total Pageviews <span id="busuanzi_value_site_pv"></span></span>
```

When a website is marked as "alive" by search engines, its ranking and importance will slowly climb.

# 6. Cloudflare Email Routing: Minimum Cost Upgrade for Professionalism

Once you have your own domain (e.g., `datafox.tw`), if you want a professional email address (like `datafox.tw@gmail.com`), you usually need to purchase Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, which incurs a subscription fee of at least $6 USD per month.

However, I discovered a **completely free** and powerful solution: **Cloudflare Email Routing**.

### 6.1 Hosting Your Domain on Cloudflare
This is the step I most recommend. You can point the DNS servers of the domain you bought on GoDaddy to Cloudflare's. This itself is also **completely free**, and Cloudflare provides:
- **The world's strongest CDN**: Allowing readers dispersed globally to quickly load your static webpages.
- **Enterprise-grade security protection**: Automatically defending against DDoS attacks, giving even personal websites a "shield" comparable to banks.
- **Blazingly fast DNS resolution**: Simpler interface than most registrars' built-in ones, and extremely fast resolution propagation.

### 6.2 Implementing Email Rerouting
By simply enabling Email Routing in the Cloudflare dashboard, you can magically create "custom email aliases," such as:
- `datafox.tw@gmail.com` 👉 forwards to my `gmail`
- `research@datafox.tw` 👉 forwards to another specified inbox

**The significance of this for a personal brand is:**
Visitors to your official website will see a professional email address with strong brand consistency, but as a developer, you **don't need to switch between different dashboards; you can handle all incoming mail directly in your original Gmail.** This is what I consider to be the most cost-effective (cost 0, professionalism +100) hidden setting.
