TITLE: Writing in My Native Language, Letting AI Take My Articles to the World: A Full-Site Automatic Bilingualization Implementation
DESCRIPTION: Leveraging GitHub Actions and the Gemini API to automatically enable Chinese-English bilingual switching for all articles on the site, without altering writing habits.
BODY:

In the process of building this personal website, I've always faced a dilemma: I wanted to **record and create in my most familiar and heartfelt native language (Traditional Chinese)**, yet at the same time, I also longed to **connect with the world, giving more English native speakers or international readers interested in my technical articles the chance to discover the potential of this content**.

Furthermore, from the perspective of managing a technical blog, bilingual content significantly aids search engine optimization (SEO), bringing a broader range of organic traffic to the website.

To solve this problem, I chose neither to spend a great deal of time on manual translation nor to force myself to change my writing language. Instead, I decided to leverage the power of AI to automate this process.

## Focus on Creation, Leave Translation to AI

I recently implemented an automated translation workflow on my website based on **GitHub Actions** and the **Gemini API**:

1.  **Maintain Original Writing Habits**: I simply focus on writing Markdown articles in Chinese.
2.  **Automated Translation Trigger**: Whenever I push a new article to GitHub, a GitHub Action automatically executes a Python script in the background.
3.  **Gemini Takes Over**: The script scans for articles that don't yet have an English version, calls the `gemini-2.5-flash` model, and automatically translates the content accurately into fluent English.
4.  **Automatic Website Update**: Once the translation is complete, the script automatically packages the content into an HTML block with Chinese-English switching functionality and directly commits the changes back to the repository.

Now, at the top of this article (and other articles on the website), you should see a "**🌐 切換為英文 (Switch to English)**" button. All of this happens automatically, without any intervention from my side.

In this way, I not only preserve the purity and joy of writing but also empower this website to reach a global audience.

## Future Plans: Moving Towards a More Modern Architecture

While the current approach effectively meets the needs of "focus on creation + automatic translation," I know that this website still has room for improvement.

To provide a better reading experience and performance, I will later continue to try migrating the website architecture to a more lightweight and faster-loading **Astro.js**. This will be combined with Astro's native internationalization (i18n / Localization) features, evolving bilingual switching beyond merely hiding and displaying content to a modern architecture that perfectly adapts everything from URL structure and metadata to interface languages.

The world is vast, and language should not be a barrier to sharing knowledge. Going forward, I'll let AI continue to help me translate these words for more people who need them!
